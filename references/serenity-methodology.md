# Serenity Methodology Reference

This file summarizes recurring patterns observed in author-only and author-attributed Serenity / @aleabitoreddit material. Use it as a guide, not as a substitute for source records.

## Core Frame

Serenity's repeated frame is AI/semiconductor supply-chain bottleneck research. The strongest recurring thesis pattern is: identify a technical architecture shift, map it to an underfollowed chokepoint supplier, then look for a public security where market pricing has not caught up.

## Common Research Moves

- Map AI demand to physical constraints rather than only to obvious GPU leaders.
- Prefer underfollowed supply-chain nodes: substrates, lasers, optical components, test, packaging, memory, and data-center infrastructure.
- Watch for policy and capital-market catalysts: CHIPS Act, national-security funding, Nasdaq listings, redomiciliation, strategic financing.
- Compare companies by quality of financing and dilution, especially in Neocloud/miner-pivot stories.
- Treat volatility as expected when conviction rests on a technical bottleneck thesis.

## High-Frequency Themes From Current Corpus

- CPO / photonics / silicon photonics.
- SIVE as CPO/CW laser bottleneck.
- AXTI as InP or substrate chokepoint.
- SOI as silicon-on-insulator substrate exposure.
- AAOI, LITE, COHR, MRVL, TSEM, IQE as optical/photonic chain references.
- NBIS versus IREN in Neocloud financing and dilution comparisons.
- RPI, AI PC, and edge/physical AI as adjacent compute themes.

## Caveats

- Track-record and return claims require independent verification.
- A post can itself create a market event in small or low-liquidity stocks.
- Browser timeline captures may include quoted-post text and metrics.
- Author style includes high conviction and rapid thesis updates; do not assume old posts remain current without later follow-up.
