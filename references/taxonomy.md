# Serenity Research Taxonomy

Use these labels to classify Serenity / @aleabitoreddit posts.

## Sector Labels

- `photonics`: optical communication, lasers, optical modules, CPO, silicon photonics.
- `substrates-materials`: InP, SOI, glass core substrate, wafers, epiwafers.
- `memory`: HBM, DRAM, NAND, SSD, memory cycle, SK Hynix, Micron, SanDisk.
- `neocloud`: GPU cloud, AI data centers, miners pivoting to compute, hyperscaler contracts.
- `ai-infra`: data centers, power, cooling, networking, Nvidia supply chain, server ODMs.
- `semicap-test`: semiconductor equipment, test, burn-in, wafer-level tools.
- `policy-sovereignty`: CHIPS Act, DoD funding, EU chips policy, national security supply chains.
- `consumer-ai-hardware`: AI PC, edge devices, Raspberry Pi, consumer compute.
- `defense-space-nuclear`: directed energy, satellite cycle, nuclear/SMR AI power.
- `market-structure`: gamma squeeze, short squeeze, dilution, convertibles, ATM, float, liquidity.
- `self-positioning`: free research, anti-paywall, track record claims, audience/community notes.

## Thesis Labels

- `chokepoint`: bottleneck asset or supplier viewed as strategically scarce.
- `architecture-shift`: claim depends on next-generation technical architecture changing.
- `capacity-sold-out`: near-term demand exceeds supply or capacity is pre-committed.
- `underfollowed-exposure`: market has not priced a company's true AI/semiconductor exposure.
- `policy-catalyst`: government funding, listing, sovereignty, or strategic industry policy.
- `financing-quality`: financing structure, dilution, convertibles, strategic investor quality.
- `cycle-rotation`: capital moves through a sector sequence such as memory -> optical -> CPO.
- `management-capital-markets`: listing, redomiciliation, governance, capital allocation.
- `risk-management`: volatility, sizing, drawdown tolerance, thesis invalidation, crowding.

## Evidence Labels

- `direct_x_status`: direct X status URL with visible author content.
- `browser_timeline_visible`: captured from logged-in X timeline, may be truncated or quote-contaminated.
- `author_attributed_secondary`: interview or article explicitly attributing a statement to Serenity.
- `third_party_summary`: tracker/media/signal-site interpretation; do not use as author evidence.
- `inference`: analyst inference from patterns across posts; label clearly.
